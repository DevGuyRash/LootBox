title=LootBox
description=Client-side looting, storage and inventory tools for Project Zomboid Build 42.
description=
description=Label your storage once. A container can take one item, a kind of item, your own categories, game categories or anything else, and be filled first, as usual or last. Container titles, icons and item tooltips say what belongs where. Put Away carries what you hold to its place, with a preview. Automatic put-away, off by default, does it when you come home and stand still, and never touches what you left with.
description=
description=Right-click anything for only the actions that apply there: take all of a type or category, loot a room or building with your loot list, haul to your car, or clean into real trash cans. Smart Take, Match and Condense fill and pack your bags. Pour and fill vessels, refill water dispensers, and eat or drink until full. Jobs walk with native actions; F7 shows the current job, and Pause and Resume keep progress.
description=
description=Standalone client-side mod with no helper dependency. Once loaded, LootBox uses the normal multiplayer actions and does not run code on the server. Native capacity, access, safehouse permissions and server trash settings apply. Cleanup deletes items through the native whole-bin trash command.
description=
description=Settings, categories, labels and saved jobs are local to each player. A name you give a fixture is the game's own container name, so everyone sees it. An unacknowledged empty multiplayer-container reply cannot certify that its contents are empty.
