LootBox 0.9.10

Single-player: Open and Eat > Til Full can now open the selected can and eat from the food it produced. In multiplayer, Open first for Til Full opens one item without taking a bite; choose Eat > Til Full on the opened food. Open and Drink also supports eligible sealed vessels that use a native opening recipe. Needed openers, selected-stack limits, fullness targets and existing unsafe-food/drink restrictions still apply.
