LootBox 0.9.11

A compact card now shows the outcome when a LootBox job ends, even with Progress closed. It reports what actually moved, including game weight when available, and tells you when items were skipped or the job stopped. Pausing a job does not show a card.

Til Full reports whether the selected need was satisfied, the stack ran out, or the requested portion was consumed. Already-satisfied requests stay quiet. Cards fade after four seconds and do not capture clicks or keyboard focus.
